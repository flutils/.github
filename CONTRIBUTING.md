
# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing to our work.

These guidelines apply to all repositories that don't have their own `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

Our repositories contain tools, systems and technologies developed to support our work. Contributions from the wider development community are welcome, but these remain our projects. We retain responsibility for their direction, architecture, development priorities and ongoing maintenance.

- **Small fixes** (typos, documentation improvements and straightforward bug fixes): feel free to open a pull request.
- **Larger changes**: open an issue or discussion first and wait for a maintainer to agree on the proposed approach before beginning implementation.
- **New features**: suggestions are welcome, but feature development and project direction remain at our discretion.
- **Security vulnerabilities**: never report these publicly. Please follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Pull requests**: keep changes focused, with one logical change per pull request. Include relevant tests and a clear description of what has changed and how it has been tested.
- **AI tools**: AI-assisted contributions are welcome, but contributors remain responsible for understanding, reviewing and validating everything they submit.
- **Licensing**: contributions are subject to the licensing terms of the individual repository. Please review the applicable licence before submitting your work.

## Project ownership and direction

Contributing to our projects does not confer ownership, decision-making authority or an expectation that a contribution will be accepted.

We retain control over project direction, technical decisions, release schedules and the acceptance or rejection of contributions.

We reserve the right to modify, maintain, restructure or discontinue projects at our discretion.

We appreciate the time and expertise contributors bring to our work and aim to treat all contributions fairly and respectfully.

Everyone participating in our projects is expected to engage constructively and respectfully with others.
