
# Security policy

This policy covers repositories maintained by Frontline Utilities LTD (Frontline) that do not have their own `SECURITY.md`, along with software and services operated by Frontline.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue, discussion or pull request for a security vulnerability.**

Report it privately:

- On GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and select **Report a vulnerability**, where available.
- By email: [security@fl.co.uk](mailto:security@fl.co.uk)

Please include:

- The affected repository, software version or service URL.
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability.
- The potential impact and what an attacker could achieve.
- Any relevant logs, screenshots or proof of concept.
- Whether you would like to be credited for the discovery.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information or production credentials in your report.

## What happens next

- We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 working days.
- We investigate the report and assess its validity and severity as soon as reasonably possible.
- Where a vulnerability is confirmed, we work towards an appropriate fix or mitigation.
- Once resolved, we may publish a security advisory and release the relevant update, crediting the reporter where requested.

We ask that you allow up to 90 days for a resolution before disclosing a vulnerability publicly, or until a fix is available if sooner. We are happy to discuss a reasonable disclosure timeline where circumstances require it.

## Rules for testing

Please conduct security research responsibly:

- Test against your own installations and environments wherever possible.
- Only test systems, accounts and services you own or have explicit permission to assess.
- Do not access, modify or disclose other people's data.
- Do not perform denial-of-service attacks, spam, social engineering or destructive testing.
- Avoid automated scanning or testing that could disrupt services or affect other users.
- Stop testing and report the issue if you encounter data or systems belonging to someone else.

## Safe harbour

If you follow this policy, act in good faith and make a reasonable effort to avoid privacy violations, service disruption or data damage, we consider your research authorised by Frontline for the purposes of this policy.

We will not pursue legal action against you for security research conducted in accordance with these guidelines, nor will we ask others to do so on our behalf.

This safe harbour applies only to systems and services operated or controlled by Frontline. It does not grant permission to test third-party services, infrastructure or software.
